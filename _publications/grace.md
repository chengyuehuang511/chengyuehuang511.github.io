Fine-tuning approaches for Vision-Language Models face a trade-off between in-distribution accuracy, out-of-distribution generalization, and adversarial robustness. GRACE addresses this through adaptive curvature regularization and feature alignment across clean, adversarial, and out-of-distribution inputs.
